# Image Asset Directory Structure

Place your website images in their corresponding folders below:

### Folder Structure & Descriptions:

- `public/images/hero/`
  - **Main Doctor Portrait / Banner**: Name your primary hero image `dr-pulak-hero.jpg` or `dr-pulak-hero.webp`.

- `public/images/bio/`
  - **Doctor About / Profile Photos**: Place Dr. Pulak's secondary profile photos here (e.g. `dr-pulak-about.jpg`).

- `public/images/treatments/`
  - **Procedure & Service Images**: Place treatment images here:
    - `knee-replacement.jpg`
    - `hip-arthroscopy.jpg`
    - `sports-injury.jpg`
    - `joint-preservation.jpg`

- `public/images/clinic/`
  - **Clinic Environment & Facilities**: Photos of StepUp Joints clinic, consultation rooms, operation theaters (e.g. `clinic-front.jpg`, `operation-theater.jpg`).

- `public/images/credentials/`
  - **Logos & Badges**: Certifications, hospital affiliations, awards (e.g. `fortis-logo.png`, `max-logo.png`, `award-badge.png`).

---

### Quick Checklist for your Images:
1. Copy your image files into one of the folders above.
2. Use lowercase letters with hyphens for names (e.g., `dr-pulak-hero.jpg`).
3. Once copied, let me know the filenames and I will wire them directly into the website components with smooth loading animations!
